Pioneering Cell Therapies for the Central Nervous System

We are a Salt Lake City based biotechnology company developing novel myeloid cell therapies. By leveraging the unique biology of macrophages, we are engineering solutions specifically designed to infiltrate, remodel, and treat aggressive solid tumors of the brain.

Overcoming the Limits of CNS Drug Delivery

Treating solid tumors in the brain requires overcoming complex physical and physiological boundaries. Traditional systemic cell therapies face significant hurdles crossing the blood-brain barrier and surviving within the immunosuppressive landscape of intracranial tumors.

Our approach utilizes direct regional administration into the brain, bypassing systemic barriers entirely. By delivering engineered myeloid cells locally, we tap into the innate migratory capacity of macrophages. These cells actively seek out, migrate through dense tissue, and clear residual cancer cells hidden deep in surgical margins, changing the landscape of CNS oncology.

Local Delivery

Administered directly into the central nervous system to establish immediate therapeutic presence.

Innate Migration

Leverages natural macrophage motility to track down invasive cells throughout tissue margins.

Phagocytic Clearance

Engineered to actively engulf and destroy solid tumor cells on contact.

Antigen Presentation

Processes and presents tumor antigens locally to alert and recruit downstream adaptive immunity.

Our Platform Architecture

A streamlined progression designed to eliminate residual disease in solid tumors.

1. Engineer

Modifying autologous myeloid cells with specific target receptors and persistent immune-activating payloads to dictate anti-tumor behavior.

2. Infiltrate

Utilizing localized administration to position cells directly where needed, allowing them to freely migrate through neural tissue surfaces and margins.

3. Remodel

Flipping local immune tolerance. Macrophages engage in direct phagocytosis and antigen presentation, turning the cold microenvironment hot.

4. Scale

Developing short, high-yield ex vivo manufacturing protocols tailored to primary myeloid cells to secure clinical and economic scalability.
